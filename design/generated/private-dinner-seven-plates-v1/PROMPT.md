# Production prompt

Create one restrained, photorealistic editorial food photograph for a private-chef website. Show exactly
seven distinct fine-dining plates arranged on one continuous warm ivory linen-paper surface in two clean
rows: four evenly spaced plates on the upper row and three centred plates on the lower row. Use a
near-overhead camera around 70 degrees with normal-lens perspective, consistent plate scale, matte warm
off-white porcelain, soft large-source daylight from the upper left, restrained Mediterranean colour,
plausible food textures and small natural preparation imperfections. Keep generous negative space and
make every ceramic rim complete and visible. No course labels, words, numbers, arrows, connector lines,
diagram marks, legend, hands, people, utensils, napkins, glasses, loose ingredients, table decorations,
logo, border or watermark. Avoid CGI gloss, excessive symmetry, duplicate dishes and impossible garnish.
