# Промпты — единый формат услуг

Built-in image_gen; один концепт, два представления. Пять референсов переданы по локальным путям: три документальные фотографии и две сравнительные доски, содержащие все предметные зоны. Первый вызов с шестью отдельными источниками отклонён ограничением количества файлов до начала генерации. Сайт не изменяется.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished, reference-driven visual proposal for unifying THREE existing sections of a Russian private-chef website, Mise en place. This is a reviewable design concept, not a new website or a generic landing page.
INPUTS: images 1,2,3 are the existing documentary photos for private dinner, private events, masterclasses respectively. Preserve the chef identity, the people, actual environments and natural photographic character; use these as photo inserts, do not substitute a new chef or fabricate a different scene. Images 4 and 5 are screenshots comparing the CURRENT INCONSISTENT three sections on desktop and phone, in dinner/event/masterclass order. Their lower zones supply the seven plates, dense canape field and exactly six culinary technique groups. Use their food and object content, but DO NOT preserve the inconsistent layout, uneven text sizes or separate styles. Make all three upper architectures follow the NEW uniform instructions below. Keep the source dishes/objects; place them on matching warm paper without a visible linen rectangle. Reference images are CONTENT SOURCES; compose a NEW unified layout, not six independent visual styles.
DESIGN SYSTEM: exact warm paper #f4efe5, near-black #0a0a0a titles, muted brown-gray #6e665a paragraphs, hairline rules #c8c0b3, small restrained brown-gold #72561f section numbers. Elegant Cormorant Garamond-like Cyrillic serif for all titles, neutral Montserrat-like readable Cyrillic sans for body. Same heading size and weight for all three sections. Same body size, inset, numbering treatment, square fine outer perimeter, one short rule next to number, and consistent structural dividers. No shadows, rounded cards, gradients, glow, ornament, new icons, testimonials, CTAs, hero, footer, device chrome or invented business copy.
