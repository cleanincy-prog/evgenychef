# Image-generation prompt set

Tool: built-in `imagegen`.

All three prompts requested a true 1774 × 887 culinary-architectural underlay on warm ivory paper,
using ultra-thin graphite/charcoal, sparse construction guides and restrained sage, terracotta and
antique brass. The generated sheets contain no words, numbers, people from the supplied photographs,
UI, cards, shadows, gradients, logos or watermarks.

## 01 — Private dinner

Two quiet apertures preserve the existing copy at left and the portrait cooking photograph at right.
Whole ingredients split into knife work, sauce reduction and heat control, then reconverge into exactly
seven courses and an intimate shared-table outcome.

## 02 — Private event

The existing outdoor live-cooking photograph is a content/crop reference only. A quiet left copy field
and right landscape-photo field are surrounded by a griddle study, four small-bite assembly paths,
rectangular serving trays, loose standing-guest clusters, tray return and a visible refill loop. The
sheet explicitly avoids a seven-course row and formal dining table.

## 03 — Masterclass

The existing workbench photograph is a content/crop reference only. A quiet left photo field and right
copy field surround one enlarged hand-and-tool demonstration, two practice stations of three abstract
participants, a double-ring feedback node, dashed correction route and long shared workbench with three
finished outcomes. The sheet explicitly avoids tray circulation and a formal dinner layout.
