# Prompt Set — Eight Style Directions

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Output: one tall desktop website concept board per direction. The live website was not used as an edit target.

Shared brief for every board: design a full-page Russian website concept for chef Evgeniy Grybenyuk in Cyprus. Preserve his recognizable identity from the supplied local portrait and use supplied documentary cooking, sourcing and private-dinner photography as visual references. Keep the same narrative: personal-chef introduction, MasterChef biography, ingredients and sourcing, three evening formats, personal Instagram inquiry and footer. Prominent copy may use only: `Евгений Грыбенюк — ваш личный Мастер-Шеф на Кипре`, `От MasterChef к вашему столу.`, `Я не работаю по меню, я его создаю.`, `Вечера бывают разные`, `Расскажите мне, чего хочется.` Avoid a centered two-button hero, repeated equal cards, bento grids, pricing, testimonials, FAQ, partner strips, gradients, glass, glows, generic icons, invented awards, metrics or claims. Square-edged, editorial and content-led; not a reusable SaaS template.

## 01 — Полевой дневник шефа

Create a warm cream working-field-journal interface. Treat the page as a chef's real notebook: map fragments, dated sourcing routes, functional pencil notes, stamps, ingredient specimens and documentary contact sheets. Make the portrait the identity anchor, then let markets, harvest and cooking decisions read as evidence collected during one day. Use asymmetric pages, irregular crops, graphite, faded burgundy and restrained blue ink. The handwriting is functional annotation, never scrapbook decoration or a rustic restaurant theme.

## 02 — Mise en place

Create a precise professional-kitchen system in black, white, stainless-steel grey and signal red. Organize the site like mise en place: dominant chef portrait, ingredient trays, tools, hands, measured preparation sequences and clean operating labels. Use strict alignment, changing image widths and long horizontal process strips; make each section reflect a different stage of preparation. No dashboard interface, fake laboratory data, QR codes, badges, equal feature cards or decorative chrome.

## 03 — Личное приглашение

Create a quiet personal invitation addressed to one guest. Use warm ivory paper, deep aubergine ink, restrained serif type and generous pauses. Each movement should contain one decisive sentence and one primary photograph; let the portrait, biography, table atmosphere and final illustrated Cyprus-villa mood appear as successive pages of a private invitation. Avoid wedding stationery clichés, script-font decoration, generic beige luxury, multiple calls to action or promotional card grids.

## 04 — Fashion editorial

Create a contemporary fashion-editorial campaign website with the chef as the protagonist. Use black, white and tomato red; giant high-contrast serif paired with a narrow grotesk; hard-flash full-body portrait, aggressive crops, monochrome close-ups and film contact sheets. Alternate full-bleed campaign moments with dense magazine spreads and sharp typographic statements. The visual attitude should feel authored and current, not like a magazine masthead copy or a generic restaurant template.

## 05 — Neo-Swiss + kinetic typography

Create a Neo-Swiss typographic website driven by a strict modular grid, black and white with orange and ultramarine signals. Use variable-width display lettering, directional arrows, coordinates, rules and numbered typographic anchors only where they help navigation. Photography should appear through purposeful apertures and sequences, with text scale changing across chapters. No stock Swiss poster imitation, illegible outline type, dashboard modules, generic cards or motion cues without information.

## 06 — Raw luxury

Create a restrained raw-luxury photo monograph. Use uncoated off-white paper, tobacco and oxblood accents, hairlines, registration marks, scan edges, soft grain and imperfect documentary prints. Make the portrait and cooking evidence feel collected, touched and edited by hand, while the overall composition stays spacious and premium. Avoid glossy gradients, beige luxury clichés, distressed faces, fake vintage branding, ornate frames or repeated cards.

## 07 — Immersive cinema

Create an immersive cinematic website as a sequence of full-width chapters: arrival, preparation, source, fire, guests and final invitation. Use near-black frames, tungsten light, deep red accents, subtitle-scale copy and one scene per chapter. Pace the long page like a film, with occasional contact strips only when they advance time. No streaming-service interface, fake player controls, arbitrary parallax, invented venue claims or decorative cinematic UI.

## 08 — Digital collage / new-wave poster

Create a high-energy digital-collage and new-wave-poster website. Use a cut-out full-body chef, oversized compressed lettering, halftone duplicates, torn photographic edges, contact sheets and sharp overlaps. Palette: paper white, black, red, cobalt, acidic green and small yellow accents. Give every section a new poster composition while retaining one coherent type system and readable journey. Avoid random sticker packs, meme aesthetics, unrelated logos, generic bento cards or chaos that hides the chef's service story.

## Output caveat

The saved PNG files are visual concept studies. Small generated text, incidental labels, supporting people, rooms and products are illustrative and must not be treated as verified project facts or publication-cleared assets. Production copy and media remain governed by the active design documentation.
