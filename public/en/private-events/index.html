Food that fits the conversation

For this format, I choose dishes and appetisers that are comfortable to eat while talking. We start with the gathering itself: what brings you together, how many guests you expect and where the event will take place. These details help us discuss the menu and presentation.

Tell me how you imagine spending time with your guests and the part you want food to play. You may have a favourite dish in mind or an idea for how to serve it. We can explore those preferences during our first conversation.

A personal menu for your occasion

The menu begins with your tastes. We discuss ingredients you enjoy, dishes you would like to offer your guests and anything you would prefer to leave out. I then think through the ingredients, flavour combinations and presentation for the gathering you have described.

You do not need to choose every dish before contacting me. Knowing the occasion is enough to begin the conversation. If you already have a specific idea, we can use that as a starting point and develop the menu from there.

From preparation to serving

My work includes selecting and shopping for ingredients, cooking and serving the agreed dishes. I begin with the menu and ingredient choices, then do the preparation that needs more time in advance. We discuss the event location and kitchen arrangements before the gathering.

During the event, I take care of the food while you spend time with your guests. If you are looking for a private event chef in Cyprus, tell me about your plans. We will discuss the dishes and the way you would like them to be served.

Tell me what you are planning

Send a message to @evg.chef on Instagram with your preferred date, location in Cyprus, number of guests and the occasion. Include any menu ideas or serving preferences you already have. These details give us a starting point for discussing your private event.
