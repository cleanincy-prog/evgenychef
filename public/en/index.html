Cyprus · Ingredients for your menu

It starts with the ingredients

Choosing ingredients is as important to my work as cooking. The freshness and quality of meat, fish and seafood shape the flavour long before I begin to cook.

Meat
I go to the village of Lofou for lamb, choosing the meat for the dish I have in mind.
Fish and seafood
I go to the fishing village of Zygi for fish and seafood, choosing for freshness and quality.
Fruit and vegetables
I visit farmers for seasonal fruit and vegetables, and a strawberry farm for ripe strawberries.
